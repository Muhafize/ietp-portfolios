Weekly Project Progress

Week-1

During our first week, we all met at a cafe where we introduced ourselves to each other and got to know one another better. It was a great opportunity to share our backgrounds and interests. After that, we met our advisor, who warmly introduced himself and gave us an overview of the upcoming steps and what we would be working on together.

Week-2

In our second week, we met at the LT and discussed possible titles for our project and what we were planning to do, with each person expected to suggest at least two ideas. Afterwards, we met with our advisor and presented the titles we had come up with. He listened carefully to our ideas, provided constructive feedback, and guided us on how to refine our project topics further. Before concluding the session, our advisor encouraged us to start working on our portfolios and reminded us that we would continue meeting regularly each week to track our progress and share updates.

Week-3

In our third week, we met with the team and discussed our project titles in detail, narrowing them down to three potential options. I also worked on the proposal for my own idea to develop it further. Later, we met with our advisor to explain our selected titles and get his feedback.

Week-4

In our fourth week, we made a final decision on our project title after a productive discussion with the team. We then began conducting in-depth research on the chosen topic to strengthen our understanding and gather relevant information. Additionally, we reviewed the proposal prepared for the selected title, which helped us gain clearer insights into the project’s goals, scope, and potential impact.

Week-5

In our fifth week, we met with our supervisor to discuss our proposal. During the meeting, we received feedback on several sections that needed correction, clarification, and structural improvement. Based on the supervisor’s recommendations, we were instructed to revise the problem statement, strengthen the methodology, and reorganize some parts of the document to make the proposal more coherent and academically aligned.

Week-6

In our sixth week, we were unable to begin the practical development of the project because we were still waiting for the acceptance of our proposal. Since the supervisor indicated that the proposal required major revisions, we could not proceed until the updates were completed and approved. During this period, we made additional minor corrections and improvements to the document to ensure it aligned with the feedback we received.

Week-7

In our seventh week, we discussed where to purchase the required materials for our project and began identifying reliable suppliers. We also checked the availability of the components to ensure that everything we need can be sourced on time. This week mainly focused on planning the procurement process and confirming which materials are currently in stock.

Week-8

In Week 8, we met in the LT to discuss the start of our project. We also watched several videos related to our topic, reviewed the materials we need to buy, and discussed the steps we must take to successfully complete the project.

Week-9

In Week 9, we officially began working on our project. We used our development board, the ESP32, and the vibration sensor to start building the earthquake detection system. Our goal is to detect seismic activity and estimate the magnitude of the vibrations. By successfully integrating the hardware and beginning our initial tests.

Week-10

In Week 10, we completed the prototype and evaluated it to identify additional variables needed to improve the sensor’s ability to detect vibrations. Based on this, we added necessary components and made corrections to enhance vibration sensing.

Week-11

In Week 11, we met at the LT and discussed the requirements for our next step. We agreed to develop a mobile application to monitor and control the system using our phones or laptops, and to design the user interface for our project.

Week-12

In Week 12, we met with our supervisor and presented the progress we had made up to that time. He reviewed our work and advised us on what we need to do in the next step.

Week-13

In Week 13, we met at the LT and developed a mobile application that can control our project. The system can be monitored and controlled using either a PC or a mobile phone.

Week-14

In Week 14, we completed our project by designing and significantly improving outer surface.
