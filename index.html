Weekly IETP Project Progress

Project Progress

Week 1

Team Introduction & Advisor Meeting

Our journey began with an introductory meeting at the Kibnesh café where we got to know each other and discussed our common goals. Later, we met our advisor, who provided valuable guidance on the expected project process and shared insights on how to organize our work effectively.

Week 2

Idea Exploration & Advisor Feedback

We brainstormed and presented our individual project title ideas within the team. In a meeting with our advisor, each member explained their proposed topic. Constructive feedback helped us narrow our focus toward feasible and impactful research areas.

Week 3

Idea Refinement & Proposal Development

After in-depth discussions, we refined our list of potential titles to three strong candidates. Each member worked on drafting a proposal for their preferred topic. During the advisor session, we evaluated the strengths and limitations of each idea for further selection.

Week 4

Final Title Selection & Initial Research

We reached a team consensus and officially selected our project title: “Design and Development of a Low-Cost Seismometer System.” Our focus then shifted to background research, literature review, and understanding the technical components required. The advisor provided feedback and encouraged us to move to the proposal refinement stage.

Week 5

Proposal Revision and Feedback

During this week, we met with our advisor to discuss the general revisions required for our proposal. He provided valuable feedback on how to enhance the structure, clarity, and overall presentation of our work. As a team, we reviewed the suggested changes and planned how to implement them effectively. This session helped us better understand the expectations for the proposal and set clear goals for the upcoming weeks.

Week 6

Proposal Approval Waiting Period

During the sixth week, we were unable to start the practical development of the project as we were awaiting approval of our proposal. The supervisor indicated that major revisions were required, so we could not proceed until the necessary updates were completed and accepted. In the meantime, we made additional minor corrections and improvements to ensure the proposal fully aligned with the feedback provided.

Week 7

Material Sourcing and Procurement Planning

In Week 7, we focused on identifying where to purchase the materials required for our project. We researched reliable suppliers and checked the availability of the necessary components to ensure timely procurement. This week mainly involved planning the purchasing process and confirming which materials were currently in stock.

Week 8

Project Initiation and Planning

During the eighth week, we met in the lecture theatre (LT) to discuss the official start of our project. We watched instructional videos related to our topic, reviewed the materials to be purchased, and discussed the steps required to successfully complete the project. This meeting helped us clarify roles, expectations, and the overall workflow.

Week 9

Project Implementation and Initial Testing

In Week 9, we officially began implementing the project. We worked with the ESP32 development board and a vibration sensor to start building the earthquake detection system. Our objective was to detect seismic vibrations and estimate their magnitude. By integrating the hardware components and performing initial tests, we made meaningful progress toward completing the project.
